About

I build statistical and computational methods that help turn genetic and clinical data into better medical decisions.

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at SIMIS and Fudan University, and a graduate research assistant in the lab of Dr. Bingjie Li at the School of Public Health, Tongji Medical College, Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My background is in statistics and mathematics, and my recent work spans single-cell analysis, genomic variant detection, survival modeling, and large population cohorts.

Research

  • Cross-ancestry genetic and multi-omics data

    Surveying public genetic and multi-omics resources across ancestries, so studies of disease risk are not limited to one population.

  • Clinical language models for cardiovascular disease

    Studying how large language models can support clinical work in cardiovascular care.

  • Manifold fitting for single-cell RNA-seq

    Applying CellScope, a manifold-fitting framework for single-cell data, to real datasets on high-performance computing systems.

  • Benchmarking mosaic variant detection

    Simulation and benchmarking pipelines that test how well current tools find mosaic variants in sequencing data.

  • Survival modeling in UK Biobank

    An ensemble of Cox proportional hazards models for disease risk prediction in a large population cohort.
