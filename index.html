About

I am a PhD student at the Shanghai Institute for Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences at Fudan University. Broadly speaking, my research centers on developing mathematical, statistical, and computational methods for the analysis of high-dimensional -omics data.

I am currently involved in several large-scale projects, including a global cohort/biobank resource directory for population research and a clinical large language model for cardiovascular disease, among others.
