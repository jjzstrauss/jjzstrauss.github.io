About

Welcome to my personal page!

I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the Shanghai Institute for Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences (hereafter, SIMIS) at Fudan University. Under the supervision of Prof. Bingjie Li, my research focuses on developing mathematical, statistical, and computational methods for analyzing high-dimensional -omics data, with a particular emphasis on single-cell -omics.

Prior to coming to Shanghai, I was a bioinformatician in the Breuss Lab in the Department of Pediatrics at the University of Colorado School of Medicine, where I worked on projects involving the simulation of mosaic variants in humans and the analysis of single-cell ATAC and RNA sequencing data in trisomy 21 (Down syndrome).

I am currently involved in several large-scale projects, including a global cohort and biobank resource directory for population research and a clinical large language model for cardiovascular disease. For more details, please see the Projects tab.
